Selected work
======

<div class="work-list">

  <article class="work">

    <div>
      <div class="work__tag">STLcBOT &middot; ICRA 2026</div>
      <h3>Multi-robot trajectory planning with STL conflict resolution</h3>
      <p>Constrained Bayesian optimization and learned local cost maps, with signal temporal logic resolving conflicts between robots. Scales to teams of 50 and runs on real autonomous surface vessels.</p>
      <div class="work__links">

  <article class="work">
    <figure class="work__media">
      <img src="/images/gallery/bowconnect-uav-exp1.png" alt="UAV path planning: simulated trajectory beside the real quadrotor lab setup">
    </figure>
    <div>
      <div class="work__tag">BOW &amp; BOWConnect &middot; RA-L 2025, IROS 2026</div>
      <h3>Sample-efficient kinodynamic planning over reachable windows</h3>
      <p>Rather than sampling the whole configuration space, BOW optimizes over reachable velocity windows — producing fast, safety-aware trajectories from very few samples. BOWConnect parallelizes the search and learns local cost maps.</p>
      <div class="work__links">
        <a href="https://bow-web.github.io/">BOW</a>
        <a href="https://bow-connect.github.io/">BOWConnect</a>
        <a href="https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2025.3604738">DOI</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="work">
    <figure class="work__media">
      <img src="/images/gallery/stlbot-gv-1.jpg" alt="Ground robots navigating a motion-capture lab with obstacles, planned paths overlaid">
    </figure>
    <div>
      <div class="work__tag">MRCPP</div>
      <h3>Energy-efficient coverage of non-convex regions</h3>
      <p>Coverage path planning for irregular regions with obstacles and no-fly zones, combining minimum-turn swaths, visibility-graph connectivity, and workload-balanced mTSP allocation across a robot team.</p>
      <div class="work__links">
        <a href="https://mrc-pp.github.io/">Project site</a>
        <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22189">Paper</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="work">
    <figure class="work__media">

    <div>
      <div class="work__tag">YoloTag &middot; RO-MAN 2024</div>
      <h3>Real-time fiducial localization for GPS-denied flight</h3>
      <p>A learning-based marker detector paired with perspective-n-point pose estimation and filtering, giving UAVs reliable position estimates indoors and anywhere GNSS is unavailable.</p>
